Privacy notice

When you use the contact form, RHO asks only for your name, email address and message, so we can reply. We do not publish referral or personal information. Submitted data is kept only as long as needed and then deleted under RHO's retention practice.

Photographs and video involving children or vulnerable people are used only with proper consent and safeguarding. A full privacy policy and terms will be published here before any wider data collection begins.
