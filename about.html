About RHO

Who we are

RHO is a community-based organization committed to bridging humanity gaps. We use community programmes, psychosocial support, wellness activities, civic education, peacebuilding and partnerships to strengthen individual and community resilience.

We work alongside community leaders, government structures, civil society, faith-based organizations and other partners.

Our vision

A peaceful, inclusive and empowered society where every individual can live with dignity, access opportunities, and contribute meaningfully to community development.

Our mission

To bridge humanity gaps by empowering individuals and communities through mental health and wellness, peacebuilding, inclusion, civic engagement, volunteerism, PCVE and socio-economic empowerment.

What we stand for

DignityEvery person deserves respect and the chance to live with dignity.
InclusionParticipation and opportunity regardless of circumstances.
PeaceDialogue, tolerance and community cohesion.
CompassionEmpathy and understanding in every challenge we meet.
IntegrityAccountability, transparency and responsible leadership.
VolunteerismGiving time, skills and resources to community development.
EmpowermentKnowledge, skills and opportunities for all.
PartnershipWorking with communities, government, civil society and the private sector.

Privacy notice

When you use the contact form, RHO asks only for your name, email address and message, so we can reply. We do not publish referral or personal information. Submitted data is kept only as long as needed and then deleted under RHO's retention practice.

Photographs and video involving children or vulnerable people are used only with proper consent and safeguarding. A full privacy policy and terms will be published here before any wider data collection begins.
